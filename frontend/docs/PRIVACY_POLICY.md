# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 5, 2026  
**Effective Date:** [Upon App Store Approval]

## 1. Introduction

Haynes Dealer Vault ("App", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Vehicle Information
- Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)
- Vehicle make, model, and year
- Repair history and diagnostic codes
- Service records and notes you create

### 2.2 Search and Usage Data
- Parts searches you perform
- Supplier links you click
- Diagnostic queries
- Feature usage patterns

### 2.3 Device Information
- Device type and operating system
- App version and crash logs
- Device identifiers (for analytics only in development)

### 2.4 Camera Access
- We may request camera permission for barcode/QR code scanning
- Camera data is NOT stored or transmitted - it is processed locally on your device only
- No images or video are uploaded to our servers

### 2.5 Information You Provide Directly
- User profile information (if you create an account)
- Feedback, comments, or support messages
- Any information you voluntarily submit

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Primary Uses
- **Diagnosis and Repair:** To provide accurate repair recommendations based on your vehicle
- **Parts Ordering:** To search parts across multiple suppliers and provide referral links
- **Service Improvement:** To enhance app features and user experience
- **Communication:** To respond to support requests and app notifications

### 3.2 Affiliate Commissions
**IMPORTANT DISCLOSURE:** When you click a referral link to a parts supplier (AutoZone, O'Reilly, Advanced Auto Parts, LKQ Pull-A-Part, NAPA, or OEM manufacturer sites), we may earn a commission if you complete a purchase. These commissions help us operate and improve the app.

- We track which links you click to measure referral performance
- Your personal information is NOT shared with suppliers for commission purposes
- We do NOT receive your payment or purchase details

### 3.3 Analytics and Improvements
- Aggregate usage data helps us understand feature popularity
- Crash logs help us fix bugs and improve stability
- We do NOT use analytics to identify individual users

## 4. Who We Share Your Information With

### 4.1 Third-Party Service Providers
We may share information with services that help us operate the app:

- **Backend Infrastructure:** Amazon Web Services (AWS) or similar cloud providers
- **API Providers:** NHTSA (VIN decoding), OBD-II databases
- **Analytics:** Only aggregated, anonymized usage data (in development environment)

### 4.2 Parts Suppliers
When you click a referral link:
- You are redirected to the supplier's website
- The supplier's own privacy policy then applies
- We do NOT transmit your VIN, diagnostic codes, or personal data to suppliers
- Suppliers may collect data through their own tracking (cookies, pixels)

### 4.3 Legal Requirements
We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government request.

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Security Measures
- All data transmission uses HTTPS encryption
- Sensitive data (tokens, passwords) stored using device-level encryption (iOS Keychain, Android Keystore)
- No passwords are stored in plaintext
- Regular security updates and vulnerability assessments

### 5.2 Limitations
No security system is 100% secure. While we implement industry-standard protections, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Data Retention

- **Vehicle Information:** Retained as long as you have an active account
- **Search History:** Retained for 90 days for performance analytics
- **Diagnostic Data:** Retained indefinitely unless you delete your account
- **Crash Logs:** Retained for 30 days for debugging purposes

You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us (see Section 12).

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

### 7.1 Access and Correction
You have the right to access, update, or correct your personal information. Contact us to request a copy of your data.

### 7.2 Deletion
You can request deletion of your account and associated data. We will remove personal information within 30 days, though some data may be retained for legal/compliance purposes.

### 7.3 Opt-Out of Analytics
You can disable analytics in app settings. This will stop collection of usage data (though diagnostic and repair functions will continue to work).

### 7.4 Camera Permission
You can deny camera permission at any time. The app will function normally; you just won't be able to scan barcodes.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us information, we will delete such information and terminate the child's account.

## 9. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

If you are located in the European Union, you have the following rights under GDPR:

- **Right of Access:** Request a copy of your personal data
- **Right to Rectification:** Correct inaccurate data
- **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion ("Right to be Forgotten")
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Limit how we use your data
- **Right to Data Portability:** Receive data in a portable format
- **Right to Object:** Opt-out of processing for marketing purposes

To exercise these rights, contact us at [privacy@mechmate.app]

**Legal Basis for Processing:**
- **Vehicle Data:** Necessary for service provision (contract)
- **Diagnostic Data:** Necessary for service provision (contract)
- **Usage Analytics:** Legitimate interest in service improvement
- **Affiliate Tracking:** Legitimate interest in business operations

## 10. CCPA Compliance (California Users)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under CCPA:

- **Right to Know:** Request what personal data we collect
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of personal data
- **Right to Opt-Out:** Opt out of data sales (we do NOT sell data)
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** We will not discriminate for exercising CCPA rights

To make a CCPA request, contact us at [privacy@mechmate.app]

**Categories of Personal Information Collected:**
- Vehicle Identifiers (VIN, make/model)
- Commercial Information (search history, parts viewed)
- Internet Activity (app usage, link clicks)
- Geolocation (if you enable location services)
- Inferences (repair recommendations based on diagnostic data)

**Personal Information NOT Sold:** We do NOT sell personal information to third parties for monetary value.
